# Bugfix-Notes der Nischenfall-Kampagne (Wellen 1–36)

Auswahl der für Spielerinnen und Spieler relevantesten Korrekturen, als `Bugfix-Note:`-Zeilen für den Discord-Bugfix-Kanal (MoniaBot, siehe `CLAUDE.md`). Die Zeilen stehen auch in den Commit-Nachrichten der Commits, die diese Datei hinzufügen bzw. erweitern. Vollständige Liste aller Korrekturen: `docs/niche-tests/FIXES-WELLE-*.md`.

- `_engine` – Control changes are now consistent: only a Hero's current controller can act with it, "you control" always means current control, and a stolen Hero is a valid target even if the effect fizzles.
- `_engine` – Effects that "count as an Attack/Spell" now use the Hero's Ability level, are blocked when that Hero cannot attack, and set off Attack/Spell reactions, counters and limits.
- `_engine` – Spell Attachments no longer lie dormant on Frozen/Stunned/Negated Heroes; timed statuses expire normally even under Forbidden Curse of Aging.
- `_hooks` – Reactions and Surprises that watch for Attacks and Spells now also react to effects that count as an Attack or Spell.
- `Pinta, the Singing Ship` – Sacrifice summons offered Support Zones that the required sacrifices could not empty. Now only zones that can be fully cleared are valid (applies to all sacrifice summons).
- `Dark Deepsea God` – Tributes could be stranded or landed in the Support Zone of a Hero you no longer control; you can now return a whole zone or single cards, and only valid landing zones are offered.
- `Creepy Villager` – The sacrifice summon could leave a ghost copy of the creature on the board.
- `Shapeshift` – The sacrifice summon could leave a ghost copy of the creature on the board.
- `Steam Dwarf Engineer` – The replacement creature was placed into the wrong Support Zone.
- `Divine Gift of the Deepsea` – The "1 per game" limit was used up even when the effect did nothing.
- `Elven Rider` – It could be played without any Hero able to use Summoning Magic 1; it now requires one.
- `Divine Gift of Time` – It could be played without a Hero of the right school, including taken-over Heroes; a valid caster is now required.
- `Unwanted Audience` – It could be played without a Hero of the right school; a valid caster is now required.
- `Divine Gift of the Guardian` – It now protects every creature you control at the time of a possible effect, and requires a caster with the right school.
- `Future Tech Escape Device` – Checked the Hero's owner instead of the controller; it now works on the Hero you currently control.
- `Weakening Crystal` – The hand negation did not follow the Hero when control of it changed; it now moves with the controller.
- `Sickly Cheese` – It could be activated with no valid target; it now needs a Hero that can actually be poisoned.
- `Infected Greatmaw` – The hit counted as a creature effect with level 0; it now counts as an Attack of the chosen Hero at their Fighting level.
- `Alluring Light` – The provoked hit used the wrong source and level, and a Frozen Hero could still strike; it is now an Attack at the forced Hero's Fighting level and needs a Hero able to attack.
- `Sword in a Bottle` – The hit now counts as an Attack by the chosen Hero at their Fighting level and opens the usual reaction windows.
- `Javelin Throw` – Its hit now uses the Hero's Fighting level and is blocked when that Hero cannot attack.
- `Bamboo Staff` – Its hit now uses the Hero's Fighting level and is blocked when that Hero cannot attack.
- `Sacrificial Dagger` – Its hit now uses the Hero's Fighting level and is blocked when that Hero cannot attack.
- `Duel` – Every blow now counts as an Attack at the striking Hero's Fighting level.
- `Corpse Explosion` – It triggered by board side instead of control, and missed Heroes and creatures that were defeated without taking damage.
- `Gathering Storm` – Its Attachment lay dormant while the host was Frozen or Stunned; Attachments now keep working.
- `Intrude` – Its Attachment lay dormant while the host was Frozen or Stunned; Attachments now keep working.
- `Forbidden Curse of Aging` – It also stopped statuses from expiring naturally (Frozen and immune at once); it now only blocks removal by effects.
- `Dark Ocean` – The reaction lock hit every creature source; you can now always react to your own creatures and only opposing ones are blocked.
- `Golden Wings` – It now protects only against the opponent's sources.
- `Silence` – It now also reacts to effects that count as Spells; the obsolete "that is not a Creature" wording was removed.
- `Chaorc Interception` – Looked at the board side instead of who controls the target; it now follows control.
- `MOE Shield` – Looked at the board side instead of who controls the target, and ignored effects that count as Spells.
- `Cute Familiar` – Chose Heroes by board side; it now uses the Heroes you control and their Charme level.
- `Dream World Switcheroo` – Looked at the board side instead of who controls the target; it now follows control.
- `Very Special Prisoner` – A taken-over opposing Hero was not accepted as host for Divinity.
- `Rioting Village` – The "1 Attack per turn" limit now also counts effects that count as an Attack.
- `Berserk` – The Attack limit now also counts effects that count as an Attack.
- `Knife Throw` – "Attacks only" now also covers effects that count as an Attack.
- `Alice, the Puppeteer Girl` – Her damage now counts as a Spell at her Destruction Magic level, so Silence, MOE Shield, Magic Mirror and spell immunity react; a negated activation now uses up her once-per-turn.
- `Guardian Beast Hu` – The extra Action only considered your own Heroes; it now offers every Hero you control, including taken-over opposing Heroes, with their cards, Abilities and Hero effects.
- `Mirjam, the Fallen Cute Angel` – Her "1 Attack per turn" limit ignored Attacks played as bonus Actions (Quick Attack, Berserk), negated Attacks and instant casts; every Attack now counts.
- `Magic Mirror` – It now also reacts to effects that count as Spells, and negating Alice's effect no longer lets her use it again in the same turn.
- `Ghuanjun, the Undead Martial Artist` – His same-name Attack lock missed Attacks played as bonus Actions and instant casts; every Attack now counts.
- `Broghan, the Frozen Guardian of the North` – If his hit was negated (e.g. by Invisibility Cloak) the once-per-turn effect stayed unused and the hit did not count as an Attack; it is now used up and counts.
- `Teleportal` – "First Spell" ignored Spells cast instantly by other cards (Guardian Beast Hu, Coffee, Elixir of Rage ...); all Spells now count.
